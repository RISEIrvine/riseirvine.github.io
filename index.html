Remake Irvine Streets for Everyone

Welcome to RISE. We strive to make Irvine a safer place to walk, cycle, take transit in, and simply be. When residents come together, we can drive meaningful change and transform Irvine's streets to work for everyone - commuters, kids, families, the elderly and more.

Who We Are

RISE is an all-volunteer grassroots organization composed of like-minded community members who stay informed on local urban issues, attend community bike rides, and make their voice heard at the city government to advocate for better streets.

Our Vision

Vision Zero

Traffic fatalities are preventable deaths. We envision a city that has zero traffic deaths, a city where the life of a child biking to school and of a senior walking to get groceries are priorities and not afterthoughts. This means lower speed limits, leading pedestrian intervals, and high visibility crosswalks.

Equitable Mobility

A city should allow all modes of transportation to be practical, not only driving. We envision a city where walking, biking, scootering, and taking public transportation are all viable alternatives, giving everyone the freedom of mobility they deserve. As part of this, we advocate for an expansion of the bus system with high frequencies.

Safer School Routes and Youth Independence

More kids are being driven to school than ever in America because the streets have been redesigned only for high speed traffic. Irvine is starting to turn the corner. A student who bikes to school frees up their parents' schedule and gains a sense of independence. We advocate for protected bike lanes and safer intersections to cut down on school zone collisions, reduce drop-off congestion, and unlock kids' ability to get around by themselves like the old days.
